Image-Size-Fitter Support

Welcome to Image-Size-Fitter Support!

Image-Size-Fitter is a simple and powerful image compression tool that helps you reduce image file sizes while maintaining quality. All processing is done locally on your device for maximum privacy and speed.

📱 How to Use

  1. Select an Image: Tap the "Select Image" button to choose a photo from your library
  2. Choose Compression Mode:
    • Manual Mode: Adjust JPEG quality (10%-100%) using the slider
    • Target Size Mode: Set a target file size (50KB-2MB) and let the app automatically find the best quality
  3. Compress: Tap "Start Compression" to process your image
  4. Save or Share: Save the compressed image to your photo library or share it directly

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my data safe?

A: Absolutely! All image processing happens locally on your device. Your images never leave your device and are not uploaded to any servers. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data.

Q: What image formats are supported?

A: The app supports all common image formats (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, etc.) and outputs compressed images in JPEG format for optimal file size reduction.

Q: Why can't I reach exactly the target size?

A: JPEG compression depends on image complexity. Images with more detail require more data. The app uses a binary search algorithm to get within ±10KB of your target size, which is the closest possible match.

Q: Does compression reduce image quality?

A: JPEG compression is "lossy," meaning some quality is traded for smaller file size. However, at 70%-90% quality, most people cannot see the difference. You can preview the compressed image before saving.

Q: Can I compress multiple images at once?

A: Currently, the app processes one image at a time. Batch processing may be added in future updates.

Q: The app isn't working properly. What should I do?

A: Try these steps:

  • Make sure you've granted photo library access in Settings
  • Restart the app
  • Update to the latest version
  • If the problem persists, contact us (see below)
